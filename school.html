Let's Learn! for schools

English flashcards, games, printable sheets and lesson plans for children aged 3 to 6.

What the school gets

  • 219 flashcards across 18 topics, each with a picture, the English word, a Spanish translation and a spoken word.
  • Games for each topic, including listening, sorting and matching games for a whole class.
  • Printable cards and colouring sheets for games away from the screen.
  • Teacher's Corner: a lesson plan, crafts and songs for each topic.
  • Works offline once it has loaded, so it can be used on a tablet with poor Wi-Fi.

How it runs

  • Runs in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or interactive board.
  • No accounts and no sign-in. Stars are saved only on the device that earned them.
  • Read the privacy notice.
