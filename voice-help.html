Voice help

The voice comes from your device, so it sounds different on each computer or tablet. The app picks the most natural one it can find. You can change it with the Voice button on any flashcard page. Voices marked ★ sound the most natural.

Getting the most natural voice

  • Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices. Download a “Premium” or “Enhanced” English voice (for example Serena, Daniel or Ava), then reopen the browser.
  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Download an “Enhanced” or “Premium” voice.
  • Windows: open the flashcards in Microsoft Edge, which includes very natural “Online (Natural)” voices.
  • Chromebook or Android: use Chrome, which includes “Google UK English” and “Google US English” voices.
