Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This page is for parents, carers and teachers.

What we collect about children

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no way to enter a child's name or answers. We don't send any information about children anywhere.

What the app stores on the device

The app saves a few things in the browser of the device you are using. They stay on that device and are never sent to us:

  • Voice settings, such as speed and style.
  • Which topics have been finished, for the star on the home page.
  • Whether the speaker button is shown.
  • Teacher planning notes and lesson plans typed into the teacher pages.
  • The name that is printed at the top of a printed sheet, if one is typed in.

Clearing the browser's site data for the app removes all of these.

Voice

The words are spoken by the speech system built into the device or browser. Depending on the device, that system may send the text to its maker's service to produce the voice. The app doesn't control this.

Other services

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website that does this, Google may see the visitor's IP address.
  • The app doesn't use advertising, tracking or analytics.
