Privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This page is for teachers and parents.

Who the site is for

The flashcards and printables are for teachers to use with young children in class and at home.

No accounts and no sign-up

You don't need to create an account or sign in. Children don't enter their name, answers or any personal details.

What we collect

We don't collect personal data from children, and we don't receive any information about them.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps basic server logs, which can include a visitor's IP address, browser type and the pages visited. We don't receive or use these logs.

What is saved on your device

Some settings and notes are saved in your browser, on your device only. They are never sent to us: voice settings, the stars for finished topics, whether the speaker button is shown, and any teacher notes you type. Clearing the site data in your browser removes them.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when the page loads.

Spoken words come from the voice built into your device or browser. Depending on the device, that voice service may process the text.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The site sets no cookies, has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and uses no third-party scripts.

Questions

For any question about this page, email letslearn.flashcards@gmail.com.
