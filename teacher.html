How to use Teacher's Corner
  1. Pick a topic in the box at the top. Each one has a goal, a 30-minute lesson plan, crafts and songs.
  2. Show the flashcards on a screen: tap 🔊 to hear each word, and use Options for shuffle, Hide words and Hide Spanish.
  3. Print the cards or colouring sheets from the Print page for games away from the screen.
  4. Finish the topic to earn a ⭐ on the home page. Clear stars on the home page when you start a new class.
